About Me

Hi, I'm Odelya

Welcome! I am Odelya Kadosh, a dedicated mental health professional committed to supporting adults as they work toward personal growth and emotional wellness. Taking the first step toward therapy is a profound act of courage. Whether you are navigating the lasting impacts of trauma or simply seeking a more centered, resilient version of yourself, I am here to hold space for you.

Healing from trauma is a deeply personal journey, and finding a place where you feel truly seen and heard is the first step to reclaiming your narrative. My practice is built on a thoughtful academic foundation; I hold a Master of Science in Education in Mental Health Counseling from the University of Miami, alongside a Master of Arts in Social Work specializing in Crisis and Trauma Studies from Tel Aviv University. This dual background allows me to bring a unique, well-rounded perspective to our work — one that gently addresses your immediate needs while holding space for deep, long-term healing. Committed to the highest standards of care, I am also a National Certified Counselor (NCC) and an EMDRIA Certified Therapist.

My work is rooted in the belief that healing is possible for everyone. I believe in a holistic, trauma-informed approach to wellness, recognizing that the mind and body are inextricably linked. With over 10 years of experience, I provide a warm, collaborative, and highly person-centered space where I specialize in supporting adults navigating anxiety, depression, trauma, life transitions, and relationship challenges. You are always the expert of your own experience; my role is not to direct you, but to walk alongside you as a steady source of support.

To tailor each session to your unique needs, I draw on a rich blend of evidence-based modalities, including EMDR, mindfulness, meditation, and other restorative, strengths-based practices. Whether you are facing a specific crisis or simply seeking greater clarity and well-being, I am here to support you on your journey toward a more fulfilling life.

I provide telehealth services exclusively, allowing me to offer flexible and accessible care to clients residing in Maryland, Virginia, and Florida.

Choosing a therapist is a significant decision, and it is essential that you feel a genuine connection. I look forward to the privilege of potentially working together. Please feel free to reach out for a free consultation to see if my approach is the right fit for you.

My Therapeutic Approaches

Trauma-Informed Care i
Trauma-Informed Care Operating through a trauma-informed lens means I understand that your past experiences profoundly shape how you view yourself, your relationships, and the world today. In our work together, we move away from labeling symptoms as personal flaws and instead focus on understanding how your history has shaped your present. This approach prioritizes your physical and emotional safety, autonomy, and collaboration above all else. You will never be rushed or pressured to share more than you are ready for. Instead, we work at the pace of your nervous system, honoring the survival strategies that got you here while safely building new pathways toward lasting healing and independence.
Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) i
Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR) is a specialized, evidence-based therapy designed to help your brain heal from traumatic memories, anxiety, and distressing life experiences. Unlike traditional talk therapy, EMDR uses gentle, rhythmic eye movements or taps (bilateral stimulation) to help your brain reprocess stuck, painful memories. By unlocking and rewiring these experiences, the emotional sting fades, allowing you to find relief, change negative self-beliefs, and move forward without feeling constantly triggered by the past.
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) i
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction (MBSR) MBSR is a gentle, evidence-based approach that helps you step out of "automatic pilot" and reconnect with the present moment. By combining meditation, breathwork, and gentle somatic (body-based) awareness, MBSR teaches you how to observe overwhelming thoughts and physical stress without judgment. Rather than fighting or numbing your anxiety, you will develop practical, everyday tools to ground your nervous system, build emotional resilience, and respond to life's challenges with greater clarity and calm.
Person-Centered and Strength-Based i
Person-Centered and Strength-Based A collaborative approach built on the absolute belief that you are the expert of your own life story. Instead of focusing on the negative, we highlight your innate resilience, internal resources, and unique strengths. My role is to help empower you to trust your own wisdom, cultivate self-acceptance, and actively direct your own path toward healing.
Psychodynamic Therapy i
Psychodynamic Therapy An insight-rich approach that explores how your past experiences, early relationships, and unseen emotional patterns shape your present-day life. Often, the ways we protect ourselves or react to stress are rooted in old survival dynamics that no longer serve us. Together we will look beneath the surface of your immediate symptoms to uncover the root causes of recurring relationship cycles, behaviors, or emotional blocks. By bringing these unconscious patterns into conscious awareness, you gain the clarity needed to break free from the past and make intentional, lasting choices for your future.
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) i
Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) is a practical, goal-oriented talk therapy focused on how your thoughts, feelings, and actions connect. When you get stuck in negative thought loops, it directly impacts how you feel and behave. In our present-focused sessions, we will work as a team to identify these unhelpful "thought traps" and build a concrete toolkit of practical skills you can use outside of therapy to manage anxiety, stress, or low mood.
Integrative Therapy i
Integrative Therapy Integrative therapy is a warm, flexible approach that blends tools from different therapeutic styles to fit your unique needs. Rather than forcing you into a single, rigid type of treatment, we work together to combine the best parts of various methods—like exploring your past, practicing present-day coping skills, or focusing on mindfulness. This allows us to look at the whole picture of who you are and tailor our sessions to whatever helps you feel most supported, heard, and empowered in your healing journey.

Insurance & Coverage

I am currently an out-of-network provider and do not bill insurance directly. Many insurance plans offer out-of-network mental health benefits — you may be eligible for partial reimbursement.

How Out-of-Network Works

You pay the session fee at the time of service, and I provide a detailed superbill (itemized receipt) that you can submit to your insurance company for potential reimbursement.

Before your first session, ask your insurer:

  • ✦ Do I have out-of-network mental health benefits?
  • ✦ What is my deductible and has it been met?
  • ✦ What percentage is reimbursed per session?
  • ✦ How do I submit a superbill for reimbursement?
